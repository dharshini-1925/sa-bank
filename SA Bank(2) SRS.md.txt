SA Bank – Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

1. Introduction
1.1 Project Name

SA Bank – Banking Management System

1.2 Purpose

The SA Bank system is developed to provide basic banking services through a simple and secure application.
It allows customers to manage their accounts and perform common banking operations.
The system reduces manual work and provides faster access to banking services.

1.3 Scope

The system allows customers to register and securely log in to their accounts.
Customers can check balance, deposit money, withdraw money, transfer money, and view transactions.
Administrators can manage customer accounts and monitor banking activities.

2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective

SA Bank is a banking management system that manages customer accounts and transactions.
The system stores customer and transaction information in a database.
It provides a user-friendly interface for customers and administrators.

2.2 Users of the System

Customer

Register and log in.
Check account balance.
Deposit and withdraw money.
Transfer money.
View transaction history.

Administrator

Manage customer accounts.
Monitor transactions.
Maintain customer information.
Manage the banking system.
3. Functional Requirements
FR1 – User Registration

The system shall allow new customers to create an account.
The customer shall provide the required personal and account information.
The system shall store the information securely.

FR2 – User Login

The system shall allow registered users to log in using valid credentials.
The system shall verify the user's login information.
Unauthorized users shall not be allowed to access the account.

FR3 – Account Management

The system shall allow customers to view their account details.
Customers shall be able to manage their basic account information.
The administrator shall be able to manage customer accounts.

FR4 – Balance Checking

The system shall display the current account balance.
Customers shall be able to check their balance at any time.
The balance shall be updated after every transaction.

FR5 – Deposit

The system shall allow customers to deposit money into their accounts.
The system shall validate the deposit amount.
The account balance shall be updated after a successful deposit.

FR6 – Withdrawal

The system shall allow customers to withdraw money from their accounts.
The system shall check whether sufficient balance is available.
The account balance shall be updated after a successful withdrawal.

FR7 – Fund Transfer

The system shall allow customers to transfer money to another account.
The system shall verify the sender and receiver account details.
The transaction shall be recorded after a successful transfer.

FR8 – Transaction History

The system shall maintain records of customer transactions.
Customers shall be able to view their transaction history.
The transaction details shall include the transaction type, amount, and date.

4. Non-Functional Requirements
4.1 Security

The system shall protect customer information from unauthorized access.
User credentials shall be securely managed.
Only authorized users shall access banking functions.

4.2 Performance

The system shall respond quickly to user requests.
Transactions should be processed without unnecessary delay.
The system should support multiple users efficiently.

4.3 Reliability

The system shall process banking transactions accurately.
Transaction data shall be stored safely.
The system should minimize data loss and failures.

4.4 Usability

The system shall have a simple and user-friendly interface.
Users should be able to perform banking operations easily.
Clear messages shall be displayed for successful and failed operations.

4.5 Maintainability

The system shall be easy to maintain and update.
Errors should be easily identified and corrected.
New banking features can be added when required.

8. Security Requirements
The system shall provide secure user authentication.
Customer information shall be protected.
Unauthorized access shall be prevented.

9. Conclusion

The SA Bank system provides basic banking services through a simple and secure platform.
It helps customers manage their accounts and perform banking transactions efficiently.
The system can be enhanced in the future by adding features such as online bill payment, mobile banking, notifications, and advanced security.
